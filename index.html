About NaBeS

A studio that builds, not just consults.

NaBeS Platform Services runs on two tracks at once. We build and operate our own products across HR tech, edtech, agri-tech and business operations — and we take on client work where the brief is the same problem we solve for ourselves: get a genuinely usable, market-ready product out the door.

That combination is the point. Every client build is handled by a team that also owns products end to end — support, iteration, real users — not one that hands off a build and walks away.

What we build
Web platforms, mobile apps, admin & ops consoles, and the AI-driven engines behind them.
How we work
In-house products alongside contracted client builds, run by the same engineering team.
Where we've shipped
HR tech, edtech, agri-tech, business operations, and consumer marketplaces.
